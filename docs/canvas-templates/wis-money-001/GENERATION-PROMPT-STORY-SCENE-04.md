## Exact subject — the three object masters

These three definitions become binding on scenes 01, 02, 03, 05, 06, the
Welcome hero, both Home cards and the Library card. Keep them simple: they must
survive being 287 px tall on a phone.

### 1 — Pack of football cards *(spend now)*

- **Shape:** a small rectangular foil-wrapped packet, portrait, softly rounded
  corners, sitting slightly tilted with **two loose cards fanned in front of
  it**. Palm-sized — it would sit in a child's hand.
- **Colours:** wrapper **muted forest green `#285F54`** with one **soft gold
  `#EFB63D`** diagonal foil stripe. Loose cards are **warm cream `#FFF8EA`**
  faces with a soft gold border.
- **Identifying details:** the diagonal gold stripe on the wrapper; the fanned
  pair in front; a plain abstract dark silhouette of a footballer mid-kick on
  the card face — a simple shape only.
- **Scale:** smallest of the three. ~0.75× the gift's height. In Leo's hand it
  fills the palm and no more.
- **Never changes:** green wrapper, single gold diagonal, two fanned cards,
  portrait format, rounded corners.
- **Never:** any number, letter, team badge, logo, printed name or player face.

### 2 — Over-ear headphones *(save for later)*

- **Shape:** full over-ear headphones, three-quarter view, **resting upright**
  with a gentle band arc — not folded flat, not worn, not floating.
- **Colours:** **deep navy `#10333A`** ear cups and band shell, **warm cream
  `#FFF8EA`** padded headband top and ear cushions, a single **soft gold**
  hinge ring on each side.
- **Identifying details:** the cream cushion ring against navy cups; the two
  gold hinge rings; matte soft-touch finish, no gloss, no cable.
- **Scale:** largest and tallest of the three, ~1.3× the gift's height. Reads as
  a real adult-size pair — this is *why* they take saving.
- **Never changes:** navy cups, cream cushions and headband top, two gold
  hinges, over-ear (never earbuds, never on-ear), no cable, no logo.
- **Never:** brand mark, logo plate, letters, glowing indicators.

### 3 — Wrapped birthday gift *(give / help someone)*

- **Shape:** a small **cube** box, slightly turned so two faces and the top are
  visible, with a **soft gold ribbon cross and a simple four-loop bow** on top.
- **Colours:** paper in **warm cream `#FFF8EA`** carrying a **restrained mint
  `#DCEDE6`** pattern of small evenly-spaced dots; ribbon and bow **soft gold
  `#EFB63D`**.
- **Identifying details:** the mint dot pattern; the gold cross-ribbon; a small
  **blank** cream tag hanging from the bow on a short gold thread.
- **Scale:** middle of the three. Slightly wider than the card pack, shorter
  than the headphones. Two-handed for a child to carry comfortably.
- **Never changes:** cube form, cream paper with mint dots, gold cross-ribbon
  and bow, blank tag.
- **Never:** writing on the tag, ribbon printing, party clutter, balloons,
  confetti, a second gift.

### Object-family rules

- All three share **one material language**: soft matte surfaces, gentle
  rounded edges, a single soft contact shadow each, no hard speculars, no
  glass, no chrome.
- All three sit on the **same surface plane** at the **same eye level**, lit by
  the **same single light source**.
- **Equal visual weight.** The headphones are physically largest, so pull them
  back very slightly and let the smaller card pack sit a touch more forward, so
  no object dominates. No object is centred, raised, brighter, sharper or more
  saturated than the other two.
- **No hierarchy cues at all**: no arrows, no numbering, no podium, no
  ordering, no glow on one item, no category words, no icons.
