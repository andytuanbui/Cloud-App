# Leo — Story Character Lock (WIS-MONEY-001)

Leo is the child **inside** the story of *Three Ways to Use Money*. He is not a
CloudWise guide, he is not a mascot, and he is **not Cloud**. Cloud is defined in
`docs/CLOUD_CHARACTER.md`; that document does not apply to Leo and must not be
used as a starting point for him.

Before this file existed, Leo was defined only by what he must *not* be. That is
what allowed pack v1 to render him as a Cloud lookalike. This is the positive
definition. Lock him here, once, and do not reinterpret him per scene.

**Leo does not appear in `STORY-SCENE-04`** — that canvas is object-led. He is
established in `STORY-SCENE-01` (hand only) and is fully visible for the first
time in `STORY-SCENE-05`. Because 02 and 03 are drawn *before* 05, this document
— not any single image — is the Leo reference the whole pack inherits from.

---

## Age and read

- Reads **about 9–10 years old**. Visibly and deliberately **younger and
  smaller than Cloud**, who reads close to 11.
- An ordinary child in the middle of an ordinary day. Relatable, not idealised.
- Never a toddler, never a teenager, never chibi.

## Face and hair

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Hair | **Warm mid-brown**, short, soft loose waves with a fringe falling to one side, slightly untidy. **Never black.** |
| Eyes | Hazel-green, rounder and more open than Cloud's; eyebrows expressive and mobile |
| Freckles | **A light scatter across the nose and upper cheeks.** This is Leo's single strongest identifier — keep it in every scene where his face is visible |
| Face shape | Softer, rounder cheeks than Cloud, small chin, slightly upturned nose |
| Skin | Light warm tone, consistent across all scenes |

## Body proportions

- Slighter and shorter than Cloud, with a marginally larger head-to-body ratio —
  the proportions of a younger child.
- Loose, unposed posture. He fidgets, leans, shifts weight. Cloud is still and
  composed; Leo is not.

## Clothing — fixed

- **Muted forest green `#285F54` crew-neck sweatshirt.** No hood, ever. The
  round hooded silhouette belongs to Cloud alone.
- Warm cream `#FFF8EA` t-shirt collar and cuffs showing at the neck and wrists.
- Deep navy `#10333A` everyday trousers or shorts. Navy on the **legs** is fine;
  navy on the **torso** is not.
- Warm cream trainers with soft gold `#EFB63D` laces.
- **Never** the cloud emblem, or any chest emblem, badge, print, letter or logo.
- Small practical variation is allowed (sleeves pushed up, a light jacket in a
  cold outdoor beat) but the green crew-neck stays the foundation.

## Expression language

Leo is where the **feeling** of the story lives, and this is his clearest
contrast with Cloud:

- Open, eager, a little impulsive. Wants things visibly.
- Emotion sits plainly on his face — wanting, hesitating, working it out.
- He is thinking *inside* the problem. Cloud is calm, self-possessed and
  thinking *about* it from outside.
- No sadness, no distress, no guilt at any point. The story is a decision, not a
  problem.

Same world, same finish quality, a different child.

## The one-second separation test

Cloud and Leo must be told apart at a glance, at thumbnail size, from
silhouette alone:

| | Cloud | Leo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head silhouette | Hooded, rounded | Bare, brown wavy fringe |
| Torso | Deep navy hoodie with cloud emblem | Forest green crew-neck, no emblem |
| Hair colour | Black | Warm mid-brown |
| Face marker | none | Freckles |
| Age read | ~11, composed | ~9–10, eager |
| Companion | Nimbus | none |

If a render fails this test, the render is wrong.

## Framing preference

Where the objects can carry the meaning, prefer **hands, forearms, partial
figures or over-the-shoulder framing** over a full-body Leo. All six Story
entries carry `character: "none"` in the manifest, which means *object-led at a
glance, no full standing portrait*. Scene 05 is the only frame where Leo is the
subject, and even there a three-quarter or rear-three-quarter view is safest.

## Mia

Leo's **younger sister**, ~6–7, appears **only** in `STORY-SCENE-03`. She shares
Leo's warm brown hair and freckles as a family read, at a smaller scale, and
follows the same prohibitions: no hood, no navy torso, no emblem.
